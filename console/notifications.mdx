The **Notifications and Announcements** page (`https://xroute.ai/personal/todo`) serves as the official communication hub between the XRoute platform team and your account.

## Notification Categories

The notification center tracks two core communication streams:

1. **System Announcements**: Scheduled maintenance windows, breaking provider updates, new model releases, and API deprecation notices.
2. **Review Feedback**: Real-time progress updates for **Distribution Channel Applications** (affiliate/reseller partner onboarding), credit limit increase requests, or custom resource group allocations.

---

## Filtering and Management

Use the category tabs to filter messages:

- **All Notifications**: Complete chronological history of account communications.
- **Unread Notifications**: Messages that have not yet been opened.
- **Pending Review**: Applications or tickets awaiting administrative review.
- **Reviewed**: Tickets and applications with completed decisions.

Click **Mark All as Read** at any time to clear pending unread badges across the console.
