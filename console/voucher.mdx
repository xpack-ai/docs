The **Voucher** page (`https://xroute.ai/personal/credit`) lets you redeem promotional credit coupons and track your active voucher assets.

  <img src="/images/console/voucher.png" alt="Voucher Management" />
</Frame>

## Deduction Priority

When making API calls and utilizing models on XRoute:

<Tip>
**Vouchers are always deducted before your cash Account Balance.** 

If you possess active, valid vouchers matching the requested resource scope, the gateway automatically offsets usage against your voucher balance until exhausted, preventing unexpected cash charges.
</Tip>

---

## Redeeming Vouchers

If you received an official promotion code, event gift card, or partnership coupon:

1. Click the **Redeem** button under **Voucher Redemption**.
2. Enter your **Voucher Code** in the redemption input.
3. Confirm to immediately deposit the voucher face value into your account.

---

## Voucher Asset Details

The asset table provides complete visibility over your redeemed vouchers:

| Column | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Voucher ID** | Unique internal reference number of the coupon. |
| **Voucher Name** | Campaign or promotion name (e.g. Developer Welcome Credit, Hackathon Grant). |
| **Face Value** | Original dollar amount granted upon redemption. |
| **Balance** | Remaining dollar amount eligible for future API call deductions. |
| **Usage Scope** | Applicable models or services (e.g. All Models, Text Models Only, Specific Provider). |
| **Redemption Time** | Exact date and time the voucher was credited. |
| **Validity Period** | Expiration timestamp; credits expire automatically after this date. |
| **Status** | Current operational state (`Valid`, `Fully Depleted`, `Expired`). |
| **Actions** | View deduction history or coupon terms. |
