XRoute gives AI agents and developers a single gateway to discover, call, and pay for models across text, image, and video modalities.

Instead of managing separate accounts and subscriptions for every vendor, you use one API key to access:

- **Major LLMs & multimodal models:** Call OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, ByteDance Ark, Qwen, Kimi, GLM, MiniMax, and more through unified or native endpoints.
- **Image & video generation:** Directly invoke OpenAI DALL-E, ByteDance Seedream, Google Imagen, Seedance video, and WAN video models.
- **Pay-per-use billing:** Every call draws on one prepaid balance, with per-key spending limits and transparent token/usage cost metrics.
