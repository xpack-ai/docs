XRoute uses a unified prepaid Credits balance across all 60+ LLMs, video/audio generators, and 100+ real-time data APIs.

## Credits and top-ups

- **1 Credit = $1.00 USD.**
- Top up your balance in the XRoute Console via credit card, Alipay, WeChat Pay, or crypto.
- Credits apply universally across text models, reasoning models, video generation (such as Seedance 2.5), audio synthesis, and data APIs.

## Models

- **Text & reasoning models:** Billed per 1M input tokens and 1M output tokens. Leveraging partnerships with 50+ upstream providers, XRoute delivers rates below official direct connections.
- **Video & audio models:** Billed per generated second, resolution tier, or request unit.

## Data APIs

- **Fixed per-call pricing:** Most search, financial, and social lookup endpoints charge a fixed USD amount per successful call.
- **Metered pricing:** Bulk extraction or multi-record endpoints charge per returned item, with a predictable base fee.

## Cost estimates, quotes, and charges

Discovery and quoting are always **free**:

- `GET /v1/models`
- MCP operations: `account`, `list_categories`, `search`, `get_details`, `batch_quote`
- CLI commands: `xroute account`, `xroute search`, `xroute details`, `xroute quote --json`

## Quote, review, then execute

Before running paid calls in an agent workflow, request a quote:

```bash
xroute quote search/web --json
```

If the quote returns `"auto_execute_eligible": true`, the call falls within your pre-approved threshold and can run immediately. Otherwise, the agent presents the planned calls, unit prices, and total USD budget for your approval.

## Budgeting an agent workflow

Combine **per-key spending limits** in the Console with **pre-call quotes** (`batch_quote`) so autonomous loops never exceed your intended budget.
